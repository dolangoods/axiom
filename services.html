Advisory engagements, scoped to the pursuit

Four defined engagements covering the full acquisition lifecycle. Each is priced as a discrete line item, scoped against a specific requirement, and delivered by the same acquisition professional throughout.

Request a Briefing↗
CLIN 0001 · Fixed Price

Proposal Compliance Assurance

A comprehensive compliance and evaluator-readiness assessment for an active federal solicitation. Every volume is read the way a government evaluator will read it — against the stated criteria, not against internal assumptions.

What you receive

A compliance matrix mapped to Section L and Section M, a volume-by-volume gap analysis, a findings register ranked by disqualification risk, and a working session to walk the proposal team through required corrections before submission.

Price
$25,000 per solicitation
Typical duration
2–3 weeks, scaled to solicitation complexity
Best suited to
Organizations with a drafted proposal approaching submission
Primary risk addressed
Elimination on compliance before technical evaluation
CLIN 0002 · Fixed Price

Competitive Proposal Strategy

Strategic advisory for organizations pursuing high-value Department of Defense opportunities. Focused on where the technical approach and the price need to sit relative to how the requirement will actually be evaluated.

What you receive

An evaluation-criteria breakdown, a competitive positioning assessment, pricing strategy recommendations tested against realistic ranges, discriminator development, and direct advisory access to your capture and proposal leads through submission.

Price
$50,000 per pursuit
Typical duration
4–8 weeks, aligned to the solicitation timeline
Best suited to
High-value pursuits where win probability justifies dedicated strategy
Primary risk addressed
A technically sound proposal that does not compete on evaluated value
CLIN 0003 · Recurring

Capture-to-Award Executive Advisory

Ongoing executive guidance for organizations managing multiple concurrent pursuits. Covers opportunity qualification, teaming decisions, pipeline sequencing, and negotiation support from capture through award.

What you receive

Standing advisory availability, pipeline review against qualification criteria, bid/no-bid guidance, teaming and subcontract structure input, negotiation preparation, and post-award debrief analysis carried forward into the next pursuit.

Price
Starting at $20,000 per month
Typical duration
Ongoing, with a recommended minimum of two quarters
Best suited to
Organizations running several pursuits simultaneously
Primary risk addressed
Pursuing the wrong opportunities and losing knowledge between them
CLIN 0004 · Urgent Response

Emergency Proposal Rescue

Rapid-response support for organizations facing a critical proposal deadline with a submission at risk. Mobilized within days, focused on getting a compliant, competitive proposal out the door.

What you receive

Immediate triage of the current draft, prioritized correction of disqualifying gaps, pricing sanity check, direct drafting and editing support where needed, and a submission-readiness sign-off before the deadline.

Price
$75,000–$150,000 per engagement, scoped to scale and timeline
Typical duration
Days to three weeks, dictated by the deadline
Best suited to
Submissions at genuine risk of being late, non-compliant, or withdrawn
Primary risk addressed
Losing a qualified opportunity to execution failure
